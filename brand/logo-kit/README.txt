MioTEM logo kit (concept G, La Porta)

Files
- miotem-logo-horizontal.svg / -tagline / -reversed / -mono : main lockups (wordmark is outlined, no font needed)
- miotem-mark*.svg : doorway and globe symbol alone (transparent background)
- miotem-icon.svg, favicon.svg, png/ : app icon and favicons

Colours
- Forest green  #1F3A2E (logo, header)
- Terracotta    #B83B1F (globe on light backgrounds)
- Light terra   #E27A58 (globe on dark green)
- Cream         #F7F2E8
- Ink           #2B2B26 (one colour version)

Typeface
- Wordmark: Fraunces, Mio in Regular 400 and TEM in ExtraBold 800 (SIL Open Font License)
- Tagline: Public Sans SemiBold, tracked capitals

Use
- Clear space: at least half the height of the doorway on all sides.
- Minimum size: lockup 120 px wide on screen; symbol alone 16 px (use the icon version below 32 px).
- Reversed version only on the dark green or other dark backgrounds.
